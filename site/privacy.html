PRIVACY POLICY

Eleven Deep

Last updated 20 September 2026

The short version. Eleven Deep has no server and no accounts. Your career is saved on your device. Nothing is collected about you, and nothing is sent anywhere — unless you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, in which case three numbers go to Google so they can appear on a leaderboard.

Who this is from

Eleven Deep is made by an independent developer. You can reach me at nelsonkenzotamashiro@gmail.com about anything on this page.

What stays on your device

Everything the game itself needs. Your career — the world, the clubs, the players, the table, your squad and your history — is stored in the app's own storage on your phone. It is never uploaded, and I cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Your settings, such as whether matches play out or resolve instantly, are stored the same way.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-ups or profiles of its own
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, because none is collected
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites

Google Play Games leaderboards

The game offers three optional leaderboards. They only do anything if you sign in to Google Play Games. If you decline, or never tap the leaderboards button, nothing is transmitted and the game is otherwise identical.

If you do sign in, the following is sent to Google, not to me:

  • The number of matches you have played
  • The number of seasons you have completed
  • Your longest run at a single club, in seasons

Google associates those numbers with your Play Games profile, and your profile name and avatar are shown next to your score to other players on that leaderboard. That is the point of a leaderboard, and it is the only data the game ever publishes. Nothing about your squad, your results or your save is sent.

I receive no personal data from this. I do not run a server, I hold no copy of these scores, and I cannot identify you from them. Google's handling of your Play Games profile is covered by the Google Privacy Policy, and you can review or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app.

Permissions requested at sign-in

Google's sign-in screen asks for permission to manage your Play Games activity, which is what allows a score to be posted. It may also ask for access to an application-private folder in your Google Drive — this is a standard part of the Play Games sign-in and exists for cloud-saved games. Eleven Deep does not use cloud saves and stores nothing in your Drive.

Children

Eleven Deep is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes your career and settings from your device. Leaderboard scores are held by Google: you can remove them from the Google Play Games app under your profile's settings, which deletes your entry from every game's leaderboards.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something this page does not describe, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
