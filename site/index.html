ELEVEN DEEP

A club, a squad and a season at a time.

Eleven Deep is a football management simulation for Android. Pick a club, pick your eleven, and play a season a round at a time — league and cup — while the squad ages around you.

What it is

Matches are simulated from the players actually on the pitch: their attributes, their fitness, their form, and the shape you picked. Put a goalkeeper at centre forward and the attack rating falls before you kick off. Watch a match play out minute by minute, or take the result instantly.

Between seasons the transfer window is yours to work in. Bid for players, answer the offers other clubs make for yours, renew contracts that are running down, send a blocked prospect out on loan, and promote from the academy — all through the same rules the AI clubs play by.

The board is watching where you finish. One bad season is survivable. Two are not.

In the game

  • Formation and team selection, with live attack, midfield and defence ratings
  • Sixteen attributes per player, and scouting that reveals potential over time
  • Development driven by playing time — a prospect who plays pulls clear of one who does not
  • Injuries, suspensions, fatigue and rotation
  • Club finances: wages, transfers, gate receipts, prize money
  • Two divisions with promotion and relegation, plus a knockout cup
  • Careers save after every round and resume exactly where you left them

Leaderboards

Three optional Google Play Games leaderboards rank matches played, seasons played and your longest run at one club. The first two count across every career you have ever started; the third is the best single one, so getting sacked is what ends a run. Signing in is entirely optional — the game plays the same either way.

Your data

Your career is saved on your device and nowhere else. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a leaderboard score, and only if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games. The privacy policy says so in more detail.
